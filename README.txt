Bring your 868 MHz ventilation system into Homey through a ramses_esp gateway: set the fan mode, start a boost, reset the filter counter and see CO₂, humidity, temperatures and bypass position of your heat recovery unit. Physical remotes become flow triggers, room sensors become Homey devices, and every packet on the bus can start a flow.

The app connects to the MQTT broker your ramses_esp already uses, for example the MQTT Broker app on Homey. It finds the gateway by itself, scans the bus for devices and shows live traffic with decoded values in the app settings.

Unofficial community app; not made or endorsed by the manufacturers of the devices or by the ramses_esp authors.
